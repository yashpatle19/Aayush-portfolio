Ask a question ↗
What does account management include?

Depending on the agreed scope, it can include account planning, profile improvements, organizing client-supplied content, publishing coordination, agreed engagement, and reporting. Deliverables are defined before we begin.

Do you manage both LinkedIn and X?

Yes. We can manage one platform or coordinate publishing and account activity across both.

Do you create the posts?

Content creation is not part of my services. You provide the posts and media, and I coordinate the approved publishing schedule and agreed account activities.

How do you handle publishing?

We agree on a schedule and review process. I organize the content you provide and publish it after you confirm it is ready.

Do you guarantee follower growth?

No. Follower growth, reach, leads, and virality depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed. The focus is on consistent, relevant work and learning from performance.

How is pricing determined?

Proposals depend on the number of platforms, publishing cadence, scheduling responsibilities, engagement scope, reporting frequency, and account requirements.

How do you handle account access?

Access needs are discussed before work starts. We use available platform permissions where practical and agree on a secure approach; please never send passwords in this form.

Can you work with an existing strategy?

Yes. We can work within a strategy you already have, review it together, or build a practical plan from the ground up.

Do you provide reports?

Reporting can be included at an agreed frequency, with relevant platform signals and observations used to guide future content.

How can I get started?

Share a little about your account and goals using the inquiry form. I’ll follow up to understand the fit and discuss a custom scope.
